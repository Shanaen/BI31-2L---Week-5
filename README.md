# Transcriptomics Differential Expression Analysis
## Cohort
The analysis used the selected project_id cohort containing tumor and normal samples.
## Design
RNA-seq gene expression was compared between tumor and normal sample groups.
## Filter
Genes with an adjusted p-value (padj) < 0.05 were considered statistically significant.
## Contrast
The contrast compared tumor samples against normal samples.
## Selected Gene
- Ensembl ID: ENSG00000113946.4
- log2 Fold Change: 6.276919
- Adjusted p-value: 1.48E-13
- Direction: Higher estimated expression in tumor samples
## Interpretation
In this selected project_id cohort, ENSG00000113946.4 had higher estimated expression in tumor samples than in normal samples. This analysis alone does not establish the gene as a diagnostic marker, driver, treatment target, or causal gene.
## Limitations
The result is specific to the selected cohort and analysis. A statistically significant difference in expression does not by itself establish causation, diagnostic usefulness, or suitability as a treatment target.
